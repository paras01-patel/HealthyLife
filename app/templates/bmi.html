What is BMI?

Body Mass Index (BMI) is a measurement that helps determine whether your body weight is healthy according to your height. It is widely used by doctors and health professionals for basic health assessment.

BMI Formula

BMI = Weight (kg) ÷ Height² (m²)

BMI Categories

BMI Range Category Health Risk
Below 18.5 Underweight Nutritional deficiency risk
18.5 - 24.9 Normal Low risk
25 - 29.9 Overweight Moderate risk
30+ Obese High risk

Why BMI Matters?

BMI gives a quick understanding of body weight balance. It helps identify risks related to obesity, heart disease, diabetes and malnutrition. Maintaining a healthy BMI supports overall physical fitness and long-term health.

Tips to Maintain Healthy BMI

🥗 Balanced Diet

Eat healthy foods including fruits, vegetables, proteins and whole grains.

🏃 Regular Exercise

Do physical activity for at least 30 minutes every day.

💧 Stay Hydrated

Drinking enough water improves metabolism and digestion.

😴 Proper Sleep

7-8 hours of quality sleep helps maintain hormonal balance.

Frequently Asked Questions

A BMI between 18.5 and 24.9 is generally considered healthy.

BMI may not be fully accurate for athletes because muscle mass can increase body weight.

BMI alone cannot predict diseases but it helps identify possible health risks.
